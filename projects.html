Featured Project · Curriculum Design · STEM · K–5

Elementary STEM Club — A Full-Year Instructional Design Project

Two semesters. Sixteen sessions. Forces & Structures in the Fall, Earth & Energy in the Spring. A complete from-scratch curriculum for after-school STEM education at Grades 3–5, built using Understanding by Design from the first day to the community showcase.

Framework

Understanding by Design (UbD)

Audience

Grades 3–5 · After School

Standards

NGSS Aligned

Duration

2 semesters · 16 sessions

The Challenge

Design a complete after-school STEM program from scratch.

City Church Academy needed an after-school STEM Club that was more than activities — a coherent, sequenced curriculum that built genuine scientific thinking in elementary students across an entire academic year. There was no existing curriculum, no material library, and no template to follow.

The design challenge was to create something that would work in a 60-minute after-school window, with mixed grade levels (3–5), with materials that could be sourced affordably, and that would culminate in a genuine public showcase where students presented their work to families and community guests.

"The backward design logic meant starting with the showcase — what would students need to know and be able to do to present confidently? — and working backward from there. That question shaped every session that came before it."

The Approach

Understanding by Design, applied to a full year.

Each session was built using the UbD three-stage model: desired results first, then acceptable evidence, then the learning plan. Big ideas and essential questions anchored each week. Performance tasks served as the Stage 2 evidence.

Mayer's CTML principles informed the multimedia design throughout: slide decks were built to minimize extraneous load, recording sheets were structured to scaffold thinking rather than just collect answers, and every session included an explicit exit reflection connecting the day's activity to a real-world problem.

Deliverables

  • 16 UbD lesson plans (Fall + Spring) — each with Stage 1–3 documentation, NGSS alignment, differentiation, and rubric
  • 16 slide decks — designed using Mayer's CTML principles, color-coded by unit theme
  • Fall and Spring student recording sheets — one per session with exit reflections
  • Fall and Spring challenge cards — print-and-cut with mission, materials, criteria, and engineering hint
  • Fall and Spring activity planners — complete program overview with NGSS alignment and materials lists
  • Parent welcome letters, budget worksheet, and enrollment materials
  • Program website — 5-page HTML site built for Network Solutions
  • Enrollment flyers, certificates of completion, and showcase materials

"What I would do differently: the community representation framing for each artifact needed more intentional design. Naming the community served and connecting design decisions to culturally responsive practice is the revision I am currently working through."
