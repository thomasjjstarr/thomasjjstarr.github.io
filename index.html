What I Do

Learning design that
starts with the learner.

Every project begins with the same question: what does the learner need to understand, and what's the clearest path to get them there? The framework changes. The question doesn't.

01 🗺

Curriculum Design

Full-semester learning experiences built around clear outcomes, curated evidence, and sequences that build toward understanding — not just coverage.

02 📊

Assessment & Evaluation

Performance tasks, rubrics, and feedback systems designed backward from desired results. Evaluation that tells learners and instructors something true and useful.

03 🎨

Multimedia & Visual Design

Slides, student materials, and digital content designed according to Mayer's cognitive principles — coherent, signaled, and built to reduce extraneous load.

04 ♿

Accessible & Equitable Design

DEI-centered design that goes beyond compliance — materials that genuinely serve diverse learners rather than accommodating them as an afterthought.

05 ⚙

Project Management

Scope, schedule, risk, and communication planning for instructional design projects — keeping complex, multi-deliverable programs on track from kickoff to close.

06 🔬

STEM Education

K–5 science and engineering curriculum grounded in NGSS, hands-on investigation, and the engineering design process — built for real classrooms and real students.
